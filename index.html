How Color Mixing Works

🎨 Subtractive Pigment Color Fusion

The dynamo color mixer blends the two loaded sector colors using subtractive CMY light absorption, exactly like physical paint, ink, and dye mixing.

Key Formula: Combining primary pigment colors yields intuitive physical results: Electric Blue + Sunshine Yellow blends into vibrant Green, Crimson Red + Sunshine Yellow creates Orange, and Red + Blue yields Purple!
