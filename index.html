Color Theory Made Simple

🎨 The 3 Base Rules

Red, Yellow, and Blue make all colors. Mix two to make a new one:

+ ➜
Red + Yellow = Orange
+ ➜
Blue + Yellow = Green
+ ➜
Red + Blue = Purple

⚡ Why Spinning Blends Colors

When the disc spins fast, your eyes see both colors at once. Your brain merges them into one smooth color!

🔬 Paint vs. Screen Light

🎨 Real Paint

Mixing paints absorbs light, creating darker, richer colors (like watercolor).

💡 Screen Light

Screens shine light into your eyes. All light colors together make pure white.
