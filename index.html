How Optical Spinning Discs Mix Color

👁️ Maxwell's Optical Disc (Additive Temporal Persistence)

When the CD rotates above 600 RPM, retinal cells retain images for approximately 1/16th of a second (persistence of vision). Instead of seeing distinct sectors, the photoreceptors integrate incoming photons additively.

Key Phenomenon: On a rotating optical disc, Electric Blue + Sunshine Yellow blends into a neutral Silver Mist / Light Grey, because their complementary wavelengths sum together towards white!

🎨 Subtractive Pigment Dye Mixing

In physical paints and dyes, each color absorbs (subtracts) specific light wavelengths. When you switch to Pigment Dye Mode, Blue + Yellow produces vibrant Green, replicating real artist paint fusion.
